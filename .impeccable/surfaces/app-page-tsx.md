# Homepage

Scope: the single-page portfolio at `/`. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads, right after a short intro).

Audience and job: builders and players arriving from X/GitHub/game links, and founders or hiring managers. Both should leave knowing he ships complete small products, and that he is also a working FDE. Action: open a project, or copy the email.

Constraints: light weight (no 3D, no constant animation), no photography section, no terminal/console/OS-UI looks, no metaphor. Craft bar: rauno.me and emilkowal.ski, with more personality and colour (user, 2026-11-15).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sleek portfolio with no metaphor. A designed object, not a template: plum-black ink, light lavender fields, one confident display face. It refuses the grey-on-white text list and the dark neon dev site. Day job first, own projects second (user, 2026-11-15).

OWN-WORLD: Plum-black ink (#1F1A26) on white. Light lavender fields (#D6D8FC) own the intro and the contact band. A plum-black band holds Work, with lavender as its light. A deeper lavender (#4B45A0) marks hovers and focus; no green. Funnel Display and Funnel Sans. No shadows, hairline rules, 14px media panels, 4px controls. Dark mode keeps the lavender fields bright and darkens only the paper to plum-black (#18151D), as the user asked.

STORY: He is an FDE at Bluecore who also builds small games and tools. Projects show the things themselves; Work gives four production stories with real figures, plus roles and the résumé; then a way to reach him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Lavender field. Header with handle left, nav and theme toggle right. Name large at 6rem, with a 176px portrait well to its right (thin ring, wall visible above the hair). Lead with the FDE role first (no "technical lead", no "coding agents" in visible copy), contact links, then the year strip: one dot per project from 2020 to 2026 with no caption, each dot opening its project in the list. The Projects heading sits at the fold at 1440x900.

SIGNATURE: Project index. On wide screens a list sits beside a sticky lavender preview. Pointing at or focusing a project opens its description and wipes its screenshot up over the last one. On phones every project shows its own screenshot.

FORM: category standard (canon), chosen after three re-rolls; seed:7ecee2826149c3d1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
